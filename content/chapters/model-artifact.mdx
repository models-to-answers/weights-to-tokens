## Package what serving needs

A deployable model normally contains weight shards, architecture configuration,
tokenizer assets, numeric-format information, and an immutable manifest tying
the pieces to one version. Integrity and compatibility checks prevent a partial
or mixed release from being marked ready.

The artifact is the handoff between the model factory and the inference system.

## Open, closed, and what is actually released

- **Closed service:** the provider exposes an API; the weights remain private.
- **Open weights:** the parameter files can be downloaded, but the original
  data and complete training recipe may remain private.
- **Open-ish:** weights are available under use, scale, or redistribution
  restrictions.
- **Reproducibly open:** weights, code, recipe, and usable data provenance are
  available under compatible rights.

Owning weights buys control, permanence, and modification. It also makes
quantization, batching, KV-cache management, scaling, evaluation, security, and
version lifecycle your responsibility. “Free to download” does not mean free
to operate.

### What to evaluate before choosing

Check data residency, licensing, derivative-output restrictions, scale
thresholds, acceptable-use terms, provenance, indemnity, expected request
volume, peak capacity, staffing, and rollback requirements. Closed APIs often
win at low or irregular volume; self-hosted weights can win when control,
residency, customization, or high sustained volume dominates.

At load time, the runtime must understand tensor names, shapes, layouts,
precision, quantization scales, tokenizer IDs, and required operators.
Checksums establish integrity; a manifest establishes that the parts belong
together.

</ExpertOnly>
