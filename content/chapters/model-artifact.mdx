## Package what serving needs

A deployable model artifact normally contains weight shards, architecture
configuration, tokenizer assets, numeric-format information, and a manifest
that ties the pieces to one immutable version.

Compatibility matters. The serving runtime must understand the tensor names,
shapes, precision, quantization metadata, and model operations represented by
the artifact. Integrity checks prevent a partial or mixed-version load from
silently becoming a ready worker.

The artifact is the handoff between the model factory and the inference
system.
