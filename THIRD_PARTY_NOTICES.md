projects:

- AI Model Academy
- Packet Academy

reimplemented concepts within one architecture. It did not merge the source
applications wholesale. The source revisions, rights basis, destination files,
and substantive changes are recorded in `docs/provenance.md`.

revision, only concepts were used and the implementation, prose, and artwork
were created independently for this project.
